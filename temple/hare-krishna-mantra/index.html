Hare Krishna Mahamantra Lyrics, Japa & Kirtan

The Hare Krishna Mahamantra is a central practice in Krishna consciousness. It can be chanted individually as japa or sung with others as kirtan.

How to start japa meditation

  1. Choose a quiet place and a manageable time each day.
  2. Repeat the complete mantra clearly, listening to each name as you chant.
  3. If using a traditional mala, chant one complete mantra per bead; a round commonly contains 108 beads.
  4. Ask experienced devotees for guidance on regular practice.

Japa and kirtan: what is the difference?

Japa is individual repetition of the mantra, often on beads. Kirtan is devotional singing, frequently in a group. Both are ways of hearing and chanting the holy names.
