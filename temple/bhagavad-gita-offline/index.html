ISKCON HADAPSAR / DEVOTIONAL GUIDES

Bhagavad Gita, Aarti & Offline Devotional Books

A regular reading habit can begin with a short passage from the Bhagavad Gita, followed by reflection or discussion with other devotees.

Where to start with the Bhagavad Gita

Read a passage slowly, note your questions and revisit the explanation. Study with guidance from a teacher or a group when possible. ISKCON Hadapsar's Gita Mahayagna programs offer a local point of contact for families, children and adults.

How to read devotional books offline

  1. Open Offline Books in the ISKCON Hadapsar app.
  2. Choose an available book or prayer and download it while connected.
  3. Open the downloaded item, then test it with the internet disconnected. Not every resource is necessarily stored locally.

Daily aarti prayers and devotional learning

The app source includes an aarti prayer section and lecture and video resources. Combine reading with daily chanting or a reminder from the aarti schedule.

Study Bhagavad Gita in Hadapsar
