How to set an aarti alarm

  1. Open the ISKCON Hadapsar app and find the daily schedule.
  2. Choose an aarti you want to remember and enable its alarm if available in your installed version.
  3. Allow the relevant Android alarm and notification permissions. Test a reminder before relying on it.
