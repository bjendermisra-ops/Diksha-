Drawing Competition

An invitation to explore devotion through drawing, imagination and reflection on Krishna's teachings.

2025 competition archive · registration closed
Art in the service of Krishna

Draw, reflect and share Krishna's teachings

The original online drawing competition welcomed school and college students, parents and working professionals. Participation was free.

Artwork themes included the teachings of the Bhagavad-gītā, Krishna-līlā, the Yamuna, cows, Govardhana and other devotional subjects.

The listed submission deadline was 31 December 2025; the original competition has concluded. Contact the temple for future announcements.

Original submission guidelines

Prepare artwork on A3 or A4 paper.
Choose a theme from the Bhagavad-gītā or Krishna-līlā.
Add a short explanation of the spiritual lesson behind the drawing.
Submit according to the rules announced by the temple for the relevant competition year.
