About Our Temple

Discover the devotional community of ISKCON Hadapsar and its service activities in Pune.

Our Mission

A spiritual home for everyone

ISKCON Hadapsar shares the wisdom of the Bhagavad-gītā and Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam through devotional singing, group study, prasadam distribution, community activities and volunteering.

Families, students and working professionals can come together to learn, serve and experience Krishna consciousness in a welcoming environment.
