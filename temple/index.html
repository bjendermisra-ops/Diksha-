About Our Temple

Growing together in Krishna consciousness

ISKCON Hadapsar is a spiritual centre in Pune that welcomes children, families, students and working professionals. Through weekly devotional programs and the teachings of the Bhagavad-gītā, we seek to bring timeless wisdom into daily life.

Our initiatives include prasadam distribution, Gītā learning, cultural celebrations, youth activities and opportunities to serve together.
