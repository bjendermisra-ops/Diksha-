A HOME FOR THE HEART

Come as you are.
Grow through devotion.

ISKCON Hadapsar is a welcoming spiritual community in Pune. Together we learn from the Bhagavad-gītā, chant the holy names of Krishna and share the joy of prasadam and service.

From family gatherings to youth activities, each program is an opportunity to connect with one another and with a timeless tradition.
