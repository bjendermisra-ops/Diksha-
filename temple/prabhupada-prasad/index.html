PRASADAM DISTRIBUTION

Prabhupada Prasad

Prasadam is more than food—it is a loving offering shared with an open heart.

Food offered with devotion

Sharing Krishna's mercy through prasadam

Prasadam is vegetarian food lovingly cooked and offered to Krishna before it is shared. Devotees honour prasadam as an expression of the Lord's grace, kindness and care for all.

Śrīla Prabhupāda placed great importance on prasadam distribution, encouraging devotees to warmly welcome guests and share the Lord's mercy through sanctified food.

Our Service

Prabhupada Prasad at ISKCON Hadapsar

Sharing meals with visitors, devotees, families and community members is an essential part of temple service.

Prepared with care

Simple preparations such as khichdi, sabji-rice, puri-sabji and halwa are made with attention and devotion.

Offered to Krishna

Food is first offered with prayer and gratitude, following the devotional tradition of prasadam.

Shared with everyone

Devotees serve prasadam during temple gatherings, spiritual programs and community outreach activities.

The sacred tradition

Mahāprasād at Jagannātha Purī

At Śrī Jagannātha Purī, the tradition of Mahāprasād carries deep devotional significance. The temple's offerings are connected with centuries of worship and with the sacred traditions surrounding Lord Jagannātha and Bimala Devi.

ISKCON Hadapsar seeks to continue this generous spirit by sharing food offered to Krishna, so that visitors can experience both nourishment and the warmth of devotional hospitality.
