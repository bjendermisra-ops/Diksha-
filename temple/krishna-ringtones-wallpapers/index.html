Hare Krishna Ringtones & Radha Krishna Wallpapers for Android

Bring a devotional reminder to your phone through Krishna wallpapers and sounds. The ISKCON Hadapsar app source includes a media collection with ringtone and wallpaper controls.

What is in the devotional media collection?

The published app catalog includes Morning bells, Evening flute, Little Krishna, Divine Flute of Krishna, Radha Krishna Aarti and other devotional artwork. Availability may change by release.

How to set a Hare Krishna ringtone on Android

  1. Open Ringtones & Wallpapers in the ISKCON Hadapsar app and preview the available audio.
  2. Use the ringtone action if supported, granting the requested system setting permission when necessary.
  3. Check Settings → Sound & vibration → Phone ringtone. Play a test sound or ask someone to call.

Alarm, notification and phone ringtone volumes are separate. A Bluetooth device may handle incoming call sounds differently.

How to use Krishna wallpapers on home and lock screens

  1. Open the media section and choose a wallpaper.
  2. Use the wallpaper or download action available in your app version.
  3. Preview the crop and choose home screen, lock screen or both if your phone supports it.

Keep the clock and notification area in mind when choosing a lock screen. Only share media you have permission to redistribute.
