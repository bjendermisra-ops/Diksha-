Stories that purify the heart

Why we gather for Krishna-katha

The Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam brings devotees together to hear about Krishna and the lives of great devotees. Each gathering is a chance to listen, reflect, ask thoughtful questions and learn how spiritual principles can enrich everyday life.

Hear about Krishna, chant His holy names and carry devotion into every part of life.

ISKCON Hadapsar welcomes both new visitors and experienced practitioners. Devotees gather for kirtan, scriptural discussion and prasadam, creating an atmosphere of warmth and service.
