ISKCON Ekadashi Calendar & Reminders for Pune

Ekadashi is a recurring Vaishnava observance associated with fasting and increased devotional practice. For Pune devotees, the important detail is not only the fasting date but also the locally applicable parana window.

When is the next ISKCON Ekadashi in Pune?

Use the Pune Vaishnava Ekadashi calendar to find the upcoming observance and its parana window. The linked calendar is independent of ISKCON's GCal but describes using the same observance rules. Confirm the date and time with the local temple if calendars disagree.

How to use the Ekadashi planner

  1. Open the ISKCON Hadapsar Android app and choose Ekadashi Calendar.
  2. Check the upcoming observance and browse the monthly planner.
  3. Check Android notification permissions and enable reminders where supported. Confirm parana independently if the app does not display it.

What does parana mean?

Parana is the prescribed period for ending the fast, usually on the following day. It depends on the location and calendar rules, so copying a time from another city can be misleading.

How can beginners observe Ekadashi?

Ask the temple about suitable fasting practices and spend time chanting, reading scripture and serving others. Anyone with medical or dietary restrictions should choose an appropriate form of observance with professional guidance where needed.
