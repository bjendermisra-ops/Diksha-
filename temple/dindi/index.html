Walking with devotion

The Wari and the Varkari tradition

Each year, devotees undertake a pilgrimage from places such as Alandi and Dehu towards Pandharpur, singing the names of Lord Vitthala. This living tradition is known as the Wari.

Carried through generations by saints and pilgrims, the Wari brings people together with humility, devotion, song, companionship and service along the road.

Important influences in this tradition include Sant Dnyaneshwar Maharaj, Sant Tukaram Maharaj, Sant Namdev Maharaj and many other revered saints of Maharashtra.

A living tradition

Journeying to Pandharpur

The historic pilgrimage passes through Pune district and onward towards Pandharpur, with communities gathering to welcome the palkhis and assist devotees. The sound of abhangas and kirtan accompanies the travellers along the way.

ISKCON's Padyatra tradition likewise emphasises sharing the holy names, spiritual teachings, prasadam and compassionate service from place to place. For ISKCON Hadapsar, supporting Wari pilgrims is a heartfelt expression of seva.

Every step can become an offering when it is taken in a spirit of devotion.
