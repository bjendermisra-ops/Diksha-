Our Bhagavad-gītā Activities

Programs intended to introduce children, youth and adults to the Gītā's teachings through participation and shared learning.

01

Bhagavad-gītā Distribution Seva

Share Krishna's teachings in neighbourhoods, schools, workplaces and community gatherings. Everyone can participate with guidance from devotees.

02

Bhagavad-gītā Recitation Competition

Young devotees practise selected Sanskrit shlokas, building confidence, memory and appreciation for the scripture.

03

Bhagavad-gītā Quiz Competition

A friendly way to deepen knowledge of the Gītā's characters, teachings and practical lessons.

04

Gītā Learning Circle

A small weekly study setting to reflect on verses and discuss how their meaning relates to daily life.

05

Children's Story & Values Class

Stories, drawing and creative activities to help children explore gratitude, honesty, courage and service.

Program participation and schedules can change. Please contact ISKCON Hadapsar directly for current registration details.
